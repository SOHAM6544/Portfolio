Day 1: Introduction to Networking

Building blocks of networking include sender, receiver, transmission medium, message, and protocol.

Modes of communication: simplex, half-duplex, full-duplex (walkie-talkies vs. video calls).

Analog vs digital signals, with digital dominant due to noise immunity.

Key characteristics: speed, accuracy, reliability, and security.

Example: WhatsApp packet journey across networks.

Day 2: Types of Network & Nature of Data

  • LAN, MAN, WAN, PAN.
  • Client-server vs. peer-to-peer networks.
  • Bandwidth vs latency.
  • Types of data: structured, observed, inferred.
  • IoT as hybrid networking.

Day 3: The OSI Model Demystified

7-layer model: Physical, Data Link, Network, Transport, Session, Presentation, Application.

Protocols: Ethernet, IP, TCP/UDP, SSL/TLS, HTTP/HTTPS.

Encapsulation and de-encapsulation.

Hands-on: ping and traceroute.

Day 4: Inside the Web - HTTP & DNS

DNS: domain-to-IP mapping.

HTTP methods: GET, POST, PUT, DELETE.

Response codes: 2xx, 3xx, 4xx, 5xx.

Lab: nslookup and browser tools.

Day 5: Revision & DHCP in Action

OSI revision.

DHCP: automatic IP management.

Static vs dynamic IP.

DHCP: Discover → Offer → Request → Acknowledge.

Day 6: Low-Level Protocols & Diagnostics

  • ARP: IP to MAC mapping.
  • ICMP: ping & traceroute.
  • DHCP Acknowledge step.

Hands-on: trace packets & bottlenecks.

Day 7: From Theory to Zynq-Based App

Zynq FPGA app using DHCP, ARP, ICMP.

Embedded-to-network communication.

Skills applicable to IoT, automation, edge computing.
